Upgrade of foundation support beams from Grade A1 (Standard) to Grade S4 (Reinforced Industrial) for the main assembly floor area. This includes an additional 4 reinforcing plates per pier and the use of high-tensile fasteners. The modification is critical to ensure the long-term stability of the heavy-duty machinery destined for this zone. Failure to implement this change will result in a 12% increase in vibration-induced maintenance requirements over a 5-year period.
